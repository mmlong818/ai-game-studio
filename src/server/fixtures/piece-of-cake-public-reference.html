Piece of Cake is a casual puzzle story about restoring a cafe.

How to Play Piece of Cake: Merge and Bake

Your goal is to merge matching items to create new ones until the board is cleared of all matches. Click the toast pan to make toast, then merge two matching pieces into an upgraded food item.

Controls

Use the left mouse button to interact with the interface. Drag one object onto another matching object to merge it.
